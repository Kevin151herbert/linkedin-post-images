The AI Act delay gave lenders 16 extra months. Some will use them to make their credit models worse.

Early in my GRC career I learnt not to rush to automate a process I hadn't done by hand. You may end up automating a problem. The AI Act's delay invites exactly that, at scale.

Credit scoring is high-risk under the Act, and the Digital Omnibus moved those rules to 2 December 2027. But a system already on the market before then only falls in scope once its design changes significantly. Picture a lender's model team: ship now, then hesitate before every fix, because the fix is what pulls the model in.

🟢 Using the 16 months to document what's already live
🔴 Rushing models out to beat the deadline
🔴 A "do not touch" sticker on a model you know is drifting
❌ Pausing the AI governance programme because the date moved

Grandfathering protects the model. Nobody grandfathers the customer.
