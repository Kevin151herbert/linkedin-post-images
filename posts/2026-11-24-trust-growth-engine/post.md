# Post 01 — Trust is the growth engine (LinkedIn, as edited 2026-11-21)

Your product doesn't have a marketing problem. It has a trust problem wearing a marketing costume.

When growth stalls, we reach for the loudest lever: a new campaign, a rebrand, another feature. But buyers, especially in regulated industries, aren't only buying what your product does. They're buying a prediction of how you'll behave when no one is watching: at 2 a.m. during a breach, when an engineer leaves with access still active, when the right choice is the expensive one.

Trust works like a discount rate. The less they trust you, the more they discount every promise in your pitch.

🔴 47 features, zero proof you can protect a customer's data
🔴 A roadmap deck that dies in procurement's security review
🟢 Board minutes that show risk is actually discussed
🟢 The incident you disclosed instead of buried
❌ Treating governance as the brake on growth

Governance is the reason anyone lets you drive.

Reputation is simply governance, delayed.
