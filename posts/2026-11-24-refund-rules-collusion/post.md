# Post 02 — A refund rule is also a price list (LinkedIn, scheduled Tue 2026-11-24 08:00 Lagos)

In Nigerian banking, "I was scammed" is about to become the most expensive sentence a customer can say.

At a fast-growing Nigerian neobank, a wave of "hacked" accounts was filed as ordinary fraud, weak controls. I pulled the data: 80,000+ transactions. No password changes. Only new devices logging in, just 5 of them, traced back to the "victims". They had handed over their logins, let a fraudster draw overdrafts, reported a hack and split the money.

The CBN's draft APP fraud rules refund only where there's "no evidence of collusion", after a 14-working-day probe. That protects customers. It also makes your investigator the last control on your P&L, because collusion is built to look like victimhood.

🟢 Login and device data you can query in hours
🟢 Investigators who rebuild the scheme, not the claim
🔴 Closing cases as "isolated incidents"
❌ Pricing refunds as a cost of doing business

A refund rule is also a price list. Someone will read it that way.

If 20 "I was scammed" claims landed tomorrow, could your team tell victims from accomplices in 14 working days?
