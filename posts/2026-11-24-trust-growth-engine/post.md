# Post 01 — Trust is the growth engine

Your product doesn't have a marketing problem. It has a trust problem wearing a marketing costume.

Buyers don't purchase features. They purchase confidence that you'll behave well when no one is watching.

🔴 47 features, zero proof you can protect a customer's data
🔴 A roadmap deck that dies in procurement's security review
🟢 Board minutes that show risk is actually discussed
🟢 The incident you disclosed instead of buried
❌ Treating governance as the brake on growth

Governance is the reason anyone lets you drive.

Reputation is simply governance, delayed.
